**KANs Flow** is a modern workspace operations platform built for coworking spaces, study spaces, business centres, and virtual offices. It simplifies daily operations through a clean, mobile-first interface focused on speed, clarity, and ease of use.

The platform brings together client onboarding, seat management, support requests, virtual office management, staff coordination, project planning, and finance tracking into a single operational dashboard.

Designed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), KANs Flow is being developed with **Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, and Supabase**, with a strong focus on scalability and future SaaS expansion.

>**Currently under active development. Launching soon.**
